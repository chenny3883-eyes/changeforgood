Meet the founder

Chenny Galano — consultant, coach, and someone who has done the inner work herself.

Change For Good Consulting was built from lived understanding: that strategy without self-awareness is incomplete, and that the most powerful growth happens when professional excellence and personal development are pursued together.

📷 Candid photo of Chenny — genuine, warm, in her element

Chenny’s path to founding Change For Good was not a straight line — and that is precisely what makes it credible. Grounded in Communication Arts and Advertising, her early career was shaped by print media and brand storytelling. She then moved through business development, customer service, account management, and non-profit leadership — capable and recognized, yet searching for something more aligned.

The turning point came through a personal mastery and coaching program that invited her to look inward. The answer was clear: her deepest aliveness lived in facilitation, education, and helping others grow. She enrolled in TRAIL’s Executive Coaching Program — and rose to serve as President of the Rizal Academy for Innovation and Leadership, leading innovation programs and transformative learning experiences across the Philippines.

“My motivation has changed. I always have in mind how we will be able to support and impact more lives, organizations, and the society. I have a bigger vision — and it excites me to do the work over and over again.”
— Chenny Galano
